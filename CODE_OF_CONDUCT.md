# Community standards

Help students and contributors learn. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements, and give actionable feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation and unsolicited promotion are not welcome.

Share only information and media you have permission to disclose. Do not post private client examples as reproductions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. For abuse or harassment, use GitHub's **Report content** controls rather than reposting the material. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These standards apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and other project spaces. They are project guidelines, not a promise of round-the-clock moderation.
